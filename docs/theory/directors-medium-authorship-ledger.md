## Question

Is the Director's Medium primarily a theory of delegated authorship, environmental authorship, or a nested model in which the director authors an environment that governs distributed execution?

## Claim classification

### Established from existing published/source material

1. The Solo Auteur Method treats the **environment** as a creative object: registries, dependency graphs, promotion pipelines, governance rules, and editorial arrangement shape what execution may become.
2. The same essay treats AI execution as **instrumental** rather than self-authoring: direction consists of specification, review, assembly, constraint, judgment, and cutting.
3. The Director's Medium passage treats **distributed expertise** as compatible with authorship only when the director retains specification, evaluation, correction, integration, and refusal.
4. SIMVLTANEA demonstrates a narrower engineering analogue: execution capabilities can expand while identity and refusal boundaries remain explicit and testable.

### Analytical decision candidate

The strongest fit is a **nested model**:

- **Environmental authorship is the governing level.** The director authors the conditions under which execution occurs: relations, constraints, admissible transformations, evaluation criteria, continuity rules, and refusal boundaries.
- **Delegated or distributed execution is one topology inside that authored environment.** Specialists, agents, performers, or tools may produce material without becoming interchangeable with the governing intention.
- **Delegation alone is insufficient evidence of authorship.** The authorial claim depends on retained judgment: choosing what should exist, evaluating what arrives, correcting it, integrating it, and refusing transformations that break the work's identity.
- **Direct making and environmental direction can coexist.** A director may personally make some layers while governing distributed execution in others; the theory does not require a clean maker/manager binary.

This resolves the apparent contradiction between “the environment ... is the creative work” and authorship “through distributed expertise”: the latter describes **how execution is distributed**, while the former describes **where governing authorship resides**.

## Negative tests

The nested model should be rejected or revised if any of the following is required for it to work:

1. **Delegation-without-judgment test:** if merely assigning work counts as authorship even when the principal does not evaluate, correct, integrate, or refuse the result, the model has collapsed into management rather than authorship.
2. **Constraint-without-intention test:** if any rule-bound production environment counts as authored simply because it constrains output, the model has collapsed into infrastructure. Authorial environment design must encode a specific governing intention and evaluative boundary.
3. **Continuity test:** if changing executor, layout, medium, or implementation necessarily changes the work's identity, then “environmental authorship” has not actually supplied continuity across distributed execution.
4. **Specialist-erasure test:** if the model requires treating specialist execution as fungible or epistemically unnecessary, it contradicts the Director's Medium passage's explicit retention of specialist depth.

## Non-claims

This ledger does **not** claim that:

- AI output is automatically authored by the person who requested it;
- specialists, performers, engineers, editors, or collaborators lose their own contribution or authorship claims;
- environmental authorship replaces direct craft;
- SIMVLTANEA establishes artist approval, historical audio recovery, exhibition readiness, or the general historical thesis;
- passing engineering tests proves aesthetic success;
- the nested formulation is approved language for a public essay.
